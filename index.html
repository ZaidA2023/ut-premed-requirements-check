Requirement ledger

Most Texas medical schools' baseline prerequisites, per HPO. Individual schools add their own requirements — always check target schools directly. In-progress courses count toward a requirement but are flagged.

All medical-school prerequisites generally require a grade of C or better (some schools set a higher bar). Credit-by-exam (AP/IB/CLEP) courses count here and are flagged — many Texas schools accept them, but policies vary, especially out of state. This is an independent study tool built from the publicly posted guidance at healthprofessions.utexas.edu, with the TMDSAS UT Austin course list as a backup for courses HPO doesn't name, not an official university tool or admissions guarantee — confirm everything with your academic advisor and each target school.
